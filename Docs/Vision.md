1. Product Vision Document

Build an institutional-grade matching engine, starting with a purely logical, deterministic, and verifiable core. In the long term, the project will evolve into a B2B financial infrastructure platform that offers transactional integrity, predictable latency, and observability, serving as a licensable foundation for fintechs or brokers needing to replace legacy systems or in-house black boxes.

2. Problem We Will Solve and Value Proposition

The development of financial market infrastructure typically falls into two extremes: fragile in-house systems that mix business logic with persistence (e.g., database triggers performing matching), or extremely costly and opaque monolithic commercial platforms. The value proposition: A white-box trading engine, where the matching rules, priority, and state are mathematically provable and testable in isolation. Its primary initial value will not be extreme speed (HFT), but rather absolute correctness and ease of auditing.




3. Users and Potential Customers


Phase 1 (Current): Engineers and automated backtesting systems (Python scripts) that will send bursts of orders to validate execution algorithms and simulate stress.

Phase 2 (Intermediate): Product developers at a fictional fintech company who will access this engine via a stable internal API.

Phase 3 (Long-term): CTOs and architects at real financial firms evaluating the platform to license its use as the core of their secondary market operations.
